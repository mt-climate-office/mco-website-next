EcoRestore Montana is a native plant restoration resource for practitioners, land managers and landowners. Its plant database filters Montana native species by habitat, soil type, restoration goal and lifecycle, and exports the results to CSV. The site also covers 12 Montana restoration goals and keeps a library of restoration resources.

The Faist Lab, in the University of Montana's W.A. Franke College of Forestry and Conservation, leads the science and answers questions about the plant database. The Montana Climate Office designed the site and hosts it. The Bureau of Land Management, Montana/Dakotas funds the work.

Species data come from the USDA PLANTS Database, USDA NRCS plant characteristics and the Montana Natural Heritage Program.

Montana joined the EcoRestore Portal, a network of state restoration sites at universities across the West, in 2026. The University of Arizona coordinates the portal. The [launch announcement](/news/ecorestore-mt-launch/) is in News.
