EcoRestore Montana helps restoration practitioners, land managers and landowners pick native plants that will take on a given piece of ground. Its plant database narrows Montana's native species by habitat, soil type, restoration goal and lifecycle, and the list it ends up with can be downloaded as a spreadsheet. The site also walks through 12 restoration goals for Montana and keeps a library of restoration guides.

The Faist Lab, in the University of Montana's W.A. Franke College of Forestry and Conservation, leads the science and answers questions about the plant database. We designed the site and host it. The Bureau of Land Management's Montana/Dakotas office funds the work.

The species information comes from the USDA PLANTS Database, USDA NRCS plant characteristics and the Montana Natural Heritage Program.

In 2026 Montana joined the EcoRestore Portal, a group of state restoration sites run by universities across the West and coordinated by the University of Arizona. We wrote about the [launch](/news/ecorestore-mt-launch/) at the time.
