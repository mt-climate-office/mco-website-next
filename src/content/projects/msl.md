Climate became a Montana Spatial Data Infrastructure (MSDI) theme in 2013.

- In June 2013 the Montana Land Information Advisory Council (MCA 90-1-404) accepted Climate as Montana's 15th statewide spatial data theme and sent that recommendation to the State Library Commission.
- In August 2013 the State Library Commission (MCA 90-1-413) approved Climate as an official MSDI layer and named the Montana Climate Office its official state steward.

MSDI themes are statewide digital spatial datasets, vetted through the Montana State Library with the advice of the Montana Land Information Advisory Council. They are the official sources of data for their topics. A theme's steward coordinates the production, maintenance and distribution of its data.
