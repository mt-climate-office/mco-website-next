The Montana Spatial Data Infrastructure (MSDI) is the state's official set of map data. It is split into themes, such as roads or water, and each theme is a statewide dataset vetted through the Montana State Library with the advice of the Montana Land Information Advisory Council. Every theme has a steward, who coordinates how its data are produced, kept up and shared.

Climate became a theme in 2013, with us as its steward.

- In June 2013 the Montana Land Information Advisory Council (MCA 90-1-404) accepted Climate as Montana's 15th statewide spatial data theme and sent that recommendation to the State Library Commission.
- In August 2013 the State Library Commission (MCA 90-1-413) approved Climate as an official MSDI layer and named the Montana Climate Office its official state steward.
